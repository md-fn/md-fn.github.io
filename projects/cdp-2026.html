Overview

A single stepper motor rotates a deflector panel to redirect a tennis ball thrown at the robot into the correct goal. A webcam feeds a Raspberry Pi 4B which calculates ball trajectory in real time and drives the Arduino Nano to position the panel before the ball arrives.

The mechanical system is complete and the panel moves correctly. Electronics and firmware integration are ongoing.

Electronics

The control board is a hand-soldered perfboard — Arduino Nano paired with a TMC2209 stepper driver. The TMC2209 runs the motor in UART mode for quiet, high-resolution microstepping without the noise of a standard A4988 driver.
