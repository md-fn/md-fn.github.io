Overview

Built for the IEEE CDP 2025 internal university competition. The task involved following a line track and picking up and placing coloured cubes into target zones. I built and programmed the full system — chassis, sensor array, gripper, camera mount, RPi enclosure, and all firmware.

The demo focused on the line-following capability, which was solid. The OpenCV colour-detection pipeline was partially working but not competition-ready by demo day.

Subsystems

Line Sensor Array Five LDRs on a custom 3D-printed frame. Analog readings fed into a PID loop that drives differential wheel speed for smooth line tracking.
Parallel Gripper Servo-driven gripper with symmetric finger travel. Designed for the cube dimensions specified in the competition brief.
Camera & Vision RPi camera mounted on a 3D-printed adjustable stand at the front. Intended for HSV-based cube colour detection via OpenCV.
RPi Enclosure Custom Raspberry Pi 4B case with a machined aluminium heatsink and 40mm fan for sustained operation without thermal throttling.
