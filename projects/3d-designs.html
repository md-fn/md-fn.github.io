3D-Printed Monitor Arm

Slides up and down an aluminium tube, rotates, and tilts — all through a 4-bar linkage designed so the centre of rotation stays near the tube pivot point. This means it holds position under the monitor's weight without needing counterweights or friction locks. Designed in Fusion 360, printed in PETG.

Motion Vertical slide · horizontal rotation · screen tilt
Mechanism 4-bar linkage — passive balance near tube pivot
