1/5 Scale RC Car Body Shell

Designed a full body shell for a 1/5 scale RC car. The brief was sleek and aerodynamic — so the whole thing was modelled around low-drag forms, flush surfaces, and a clean side profile. First time properly using the Form tools in Fusion 360, which handle organic surfacing a lot better than standard solid modelling.

The body had to be split into two parts — front and back — to fit on my Artillery Sidewinder X1. After printing both halves in PETG, they were plastic-welded together with a 3D pen and the seams filled and smoothed with UV-curing resin. Paint was handled by whoever commissioned it.
