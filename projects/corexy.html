Overview

Built from salvaged inkjet printer parts using a community CoreXY frame design. The frame dimensions and the salvaged steppers didn't match any existing printed parts, so I redesigned the critical motion components from scratch in Fusion 360 — X-axis carriage, belt tensioners, and Z-axis lead-screw mounts.

This is the machine that prints most of the parts elsewhere on this site.

Redesigned Components

X-Axis Carriage Custom carriage designed around the specific linear rail and hotend dimensions — standard community parts didn't fit the salvaged rail spacing.
Belt Tensioners Redesigned for the belt path geometry of this frame. Allows independent A/B belt tension adjustment without disassembly.
Z-Axis Mounts Lead-screw mounts resized to match the salvaged stepper shaft diameter and frame extrusion positions.
Part Cooling Custom fan duct designed to match the hotend geometry and direct airflow evenly around the nozzle.
