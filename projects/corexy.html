Overview

Built from Kingroon Kp3s 3d printer parts using the Fraxinus 1k as base design. I had to customize many parts of the design for different extrusion geometries, custom OLED screen and Rotary Encoder interface.

Redesigned Components

Linear Rail Mounts Due to the Extrusion Geometry being different had to make custom mounts for the linear rails
OLED Screen and Rotary Encoder Interface The front skirt was redesigned to accommodate the ssd1306 display and the rotary encoder.
Custom Feet Custom designed to use silicone feet to reduce vibration and improve stability.
