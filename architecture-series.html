Urban Architecture Photography

📅 2023-2024 📍 Tokyo, Dubai, Barcelona 📷 Canon EOS R5, 24-70mm lens

Series Overview

This series explores the intersection of geometry, light, and human scale in modern urban environments. Shot across three continents, the work captures the visual rhythm of contemporary architecture—clean lines, bold materials, and the interplay of shadow and light that defines city spaces.

Each location was chosen for its distinctive architectural character. The photographs aim to reveal the poetic beauty hidden in everyday urban structures, transforming familiar cityscapes into visual narratives.

Creative Vision

My photography is guided by three principles:

  • Geometry: Emphasize clean lines, symmetry, and compositional strength
  • Light: Chase golden hour, dramatic shadows, and interplay of natural elements
  • Minimalism: Remove the unnecessary to reveal the essential

I approach each location like a visual explorer, wandering until I find the moment where architecture and light create something unexpected and beautiful.

Tokyo: Vertical Geometry

Tokyo's dense, vertical architecture provided endless compositional possibilities. The city's obsession with precision and clean aesthetics aligned perfectly with my photographic approach. I spent 10 days exploring districts from Shibuya to Shinjuku, focusing on the geometric abstraction of skyscrapers and the human scale within these massive structures.

Technical Approach

Shot in RAW format for maximum post-processing flexibility. Used a 24-70mm lens to compress perspective and emphasize geometric patterns. Early morning and late evening shoots to capture directional light and long shadows.

Dubai: Contrast & Scale

Dubai presented a different visual narrative—one of extreme scale and luxury. The challenge here was avoiding the obvious tourist shots of the Burj Khalifa and instead finding the architectural poetry in the city's obsession with grandeur. I was drawn to the repetition, the glass reflections, and the stark desert contrast.

The harsh desert sun created dramatic shadows that sculptured the modern buildings. I used long exposures to capture the movement of people as abstract elements within these massive architectural frames.

Barcelona: Organic Curves

Barcelona offered a counterpoint to the rigid geometry of Tokyo and Dubai. Gaudí's influence permeates the city, introducing organic forms and flowing lines. I found beauty in the mix—the traditional Gothic quarter contrasting with modern structures.

Technical Specifications

Gear & Equipment

Camera: Canon EOS R5 (45.7MP full-frame mirrorless)

Lenses: RF 24-70mm f/2.8L IS (primary), RF 15-35mm f/2.8L IS (wide angle)

Tripod: Peak Design Travel Carbon Fiber

Filters: ND, Polarizing, and graduated filters

Post-Processing: Lightroom Classic + Photoshop

Typical Settings

Aperture: f/5.6-8 (for deep depth of field)

Shutter Speed: 1/125 - 1/500s (daylight), 0.5-2s (dawn/dusk)

ISO: 100-1600 (auto with ceiling at 3200)

White Balance: Custom or Daylight (AWB avoided for consistency)

Format: RAW (CR3) only—no JPEGs

Post-Processing Approach

Every image goes through a consistent editing workflow:

  1. Import & Organization: Log photos, add metadata and ratings
  2. Culling: Reduce to best 5-10% of shots
  3. Develop Settings: Consistent color grading using custom profiles
  4. Detail Work: Spot removal, lens corrections, vignetting
  5. Fine-tuning: Curves, selective color, and local adjustments
  6. Export: High-quality JPEGs for display and print

Editing Philosophy

Post-processing enhances the story without creating unrealistic results. I increase contrast and clarity to match the punch of the original scene, adjust white balance for consistency, and use subtle color grading to create a cohesive visual language across the series.

Key Takeaways

  • Location scouting is as important as technical skill
  • Consistent light conditions beat perfect locations every time
  • Strong composition is more important than gear quality
